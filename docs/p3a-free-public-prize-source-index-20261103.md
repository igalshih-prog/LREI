## Rights / authenticity: distinct gates

1. **Publicly locatable:** YES, 14/14 URLs resolve and the sample pages expose both product tables. Finding public web pages does **not** prove full accuracy, legality of redistributing data or an independently verified operator source.
2. **Authority:** NO. The site's [Terms of Service](https://lottery-reminder.com/terms) say that the service is unaffiliated with official lottery operators, results may be wrong and no accuracy guarantee is provided. The terms name **Lindravo Software LLC** and list `info [at] amaze [dot] website` as a contact channel; the currently visible [site accessibility statement](https://israel.lottery-reminder.com/accessibility) also names Lindravo. No explicit research-data reuse permission or upstream sublicensing authority is shown. Confirm the permission-granting party before any systematic capture or reuse.
3. **Prior rights outreach:** the 2026-10-30 outbound enquiry found in connected Gmail was sent to a **different secondary site** (`lottoishi.co.il`), **not** the Lottery Reminder contact address. Consequently it is NOT evidence that Lottery Reminder was contacted or authorized use. An actual request to its posted contact channel must be handled separately, and a sent message is not permission.
4. **Limited independent content cross-check:** for draw **#3961**, the Regular **5 + strong** category shows **29 winners, NIS 4,385 per winner** in both [Lottery Reminder](https://israel.lottery-reminder.com/lotto/3961) and an indexed [Lotoc](https://lotoc.co.il/loto/result) results page. This is **one secondary-to-secondary content agreement**, not separate first-party operator attestation, and both sites could share a common upstream source. No other tier-specific cross-check is claimed here.
5. **Formal admission:** still **0/14 newly validated and rights-cleared draws**. The existing P3 cohort remains **6/20** observed informational records, **14/20** unavailable in an approved local observation set. All 224 rows remain blank. Main P1 data freshness/protocol and P3 model-validation gates remain independent and unchanged.
